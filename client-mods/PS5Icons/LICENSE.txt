The button prompts shown in the game with a DualSense connected are based on the pack's
"Buttons Full Solid" white icons by Zacksly. They were modified for Dauntless Revived: the symbol
is filled dark, a thin dark rim is added so they read on light backgrounds, and they are resized
to 64 x 64.

"DualSense Layout.png" is based on the pack's "Controller Images / Solid Color" DualSense by
Zacksly. It was modified for Dauntless Revived: placed on the Controls screen's layout, with the
screen's label boxes and group lines drawn around it.
